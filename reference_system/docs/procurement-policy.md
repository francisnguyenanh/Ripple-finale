## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how Acme Industrial Components raises, approves and places
purchase orders with external suppliers.

## 2. Approval authority

### 2.1 Standard purchases

Purchase requests of **JPY 1,000,000 or more** require approval from the
requesting department's manager before a purchase order may be raised.

Requests below this amount are approved automatically by the procurement
system and require no human sign-off.

### 2.2 Capital expenditure

Capital expenditure of **JPY 5,000,000 or more** is referred to the Finance
Controller regardless of the requesting department.

### 2.3 High-risk suppliers

Any purchase from a supplier on the high-risk register is referred to the
Operations Director, regardless of amount. The high-risk register is
maintained by Supplier Management and reviewed quarterly.

## 3. Emergency purchases

Where a production line stoppage is imminent, the emergency purchase process
may be used. See SOP-PROC-002 section 6.

## 4. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be recorded in the exception register with a
named approver and an end date. See `exception-handling.md`.
