# Procurement FAQ

**Document ID:** FAQ-PROC-006
**Owner:** Procurement Operations
**Last reviewed:** 2023-09-04

---

**Q. My request was approved instantly. Is that right?**

Yes, if it was below the approval threshold. The system approves small
requests automatically so that managers are not interrupted for routine
purchases.

**Q. How much is "small"?**

See the SOP. The figure is one million yen.

**Q. My customer is a strategic account. Does that change anything?**

Strategic accounts have different commercial terms. Speak to your account
manager about cancellation fees and payment windows.

**Q. Can I split a large request into smaller ones to avoid approval?**

No. Splitting a request to stay under the threshold is a policy breach and
is checked during the quarterly audit.

**Q. What is the ceiling on emergency purchases?**

It is configured in the system. Procurement Operations can tell you the
current figure.
