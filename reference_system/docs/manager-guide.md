# Manager's Guide to Procurement Approvals

**Document ID:** GUIDE-PROC-003
**Owner:** Procurement Operations
**Version:** 1.3
**Last reviewed:** 2020-02-11

---

## What lands in your queue

You will be asked to approve a purchase request when it is for
**one million yen or more**.

Anything smaller is handled by the system and you will never see it.

## What to check before approving

- Is the supplier on the approved list?
- Does the quotation match the amount requested?
- Is the budget code correct for the current quarter?

## What does NOT come to you

| Situation | Who handles it |
|---|---|
| Capital expenditure over 5,000,000 | Finance Controller |
| Supplier on the high-risk register | Operations Director |
| Emergency purchases | Fast-tracked automatically |

## Turnaround

Please action requests within two working days. After that they escalate
to your own manager automatically, which nobody enjoys.
