# Finance Desk Guide — Operational Expense Review & Sign-Off

**Document Reference**: GD-FIN-2026-V1  
**Distribution**: Finance & Accounting Department Only  
**Last Updated**: August 2026  

---

## 1. Scope and Purpose
This desk guide summarizes common routing and review guidelines for staff in the Finance Department reviewing incoming procurement commitments.

---

## 2. High-Value Expenditure Review
- Financial health and liquidity management require close oversight of high-expenditure procurement.
- As a general departmental rule of thumb, all purchasing commitments of **2,000,000 JPY or higher** must be reviewed and authorized by the Finance Department prior to purchase order commitment.
- Reviewers should examine cash flow impact, vendor payment terms, and verify budget line allocation before signing off on requisitions in the queue.

---

## 3. Review Checklist
When an authorization request arrives in the Finance queue:
1. Verify vendor credit terms and payment due schedule.
2. Confirm that total gross commitment (including freight and consumption tax) fits within quarterly expenditure allocations.
3. Coordinate with department managers to ensure appropriate asset tagging and depreciation scheduling where applicable.
