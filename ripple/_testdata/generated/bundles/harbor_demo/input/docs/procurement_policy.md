# Harbor Office Supply — Corporate Procurement Policy

**Document Reference**: POL-PROC-2026-01  
**Effective Date**: September 1, 2026  
**Document Owner**: Office of the Managing Director  
**Version**: 1.0.0  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the governance principles, financial authority thresholds, and approval requirements for all purchasing activities at Harbor Office Supply Ltd. It applies to all permanent employees and authorized purchasing agents across all departments.

---

## 2. General Principles
1. **Delegation of Financial Authority**: No purchase commitment may be made without prior formal authorization according to the financial limits defined herein.
2. **Maker-Checker Separation**: Requisitioners are strictly prohibited from approving their own purchase requisitions. Every approval requires independent verification by an authorized role holder.
3. **Currency and Units**: All official purchase authorizations and commitments are denominated strictly in Japanese Yen (JPY).

---

## 3. Approval Authority Matrix

Purchasing approval routing operates in a strict hierarchical order based on vendor risk, asset classification, and purchase amount.

### 3.1 Vendor Restrictions and Risk Precedence
- **Blocked Suppliers**: Requisitions referencing vendors marked as BLOCKED are rejected automatically. No business transaction may proceed with a suspended vendor.
- **High-Risk Suppliers**: Any requisition referencing a vendor classified with HIGH risk requires explicit authorization by a **Director**, regardless of the expense category, process type, or monetary amount.

### 3.2 Capital Expenditures (CAPEX)
- Capital equipment and long-term asset acquisitions of **2,000,000 JPY or greater** require formal review and authorization by the **Finance Department**.
- Operational expenditures (OPEX) do not fall under the Finance capital committee review and are routed based on general managerial spending limits.

### 3.3 General Management Authority (Standard OPEX)
- **Automatic System Clearance (AUTO)**: Standard requisitions for items with a net goods value strictly below **1,000,000 JPY** do not require individual human manager sign-off and are cleared automatically by system rules.
- **Manager Approval**: Standard requisitions with a net goods value of **1,000,000 JPY or greater** require documented sign-off from an authorized **Manager**.

---

## 4. Shipping and Logistics Guidelines
To optimize delivery efficiency, logistics freight costs are covered by Harbor Office Supply under standard vendor agreements:
- Purchases with a net goods amount of **500,000 JPY or greater** receive free freight delivery (0 JPY freight charge).
- Orders with net goods amounts under **500,000 JPY** incur a standardized shipping and handling charge of **2,000 JPY**.

*Note: The shipping freight threshold is strictly an operational delivery incentive and does not alter managerial spending approval limits.*

---

## 5. Non-Compliance and Audit
Failure to adhere to authorization limits or attempting to circumvent maker-checker segregation will result in immediate requisition cancellation and internal audit review.
