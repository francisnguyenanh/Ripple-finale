# Operating Guide: Emergency Purchasing Procedures

**Document Reference**: GD-EMG-2026-04  
**Applicability**: Urgent Office Equipment Replacements & Critical Operational Needs  

---

## 1. Overview
Emergency purchasing procedures allow departments to expedite critical supplies where business continuity is at risk. Such requisitions are flagged with the `EMERGENCY` process indicator.

---

## 2. Emergency Routing Rules
1. **Vendor Risk Controls**: Emergency status does NOT bypass vendor risk policies. Blocked vendors remain strictly rejected, and high-risk suppliers continue to require Director approval.
2. **Capital Expenditure Controls**: High-value capital expenditures (CAPEX) remain subject to standard Finance review.
3. **Approval Sign-off**:
   - For routine operational replacements, buyers should obtain appropriate sign-off from operational leadership before final order dispatch.
   - Low-value urgent items may clear automatically to prevent operational disruption, while higher-value orders require manager sign-off.
   - Requisitioners are reminded that emergency orders undergo retrospective monthly audit to ensure adherence to operational spend standards.
